# English FAL Paper 2 — Macbeth & Poetry Revision

A self-contained, offline revision website for the Grade 12 **English First Additional Language** Paper 2, covering **Macbeth** and **Poetry** only (no novels or short stories).

Built from an analysis of five official DBE November papers and marking guidelines (2021–2025). Includes past-paper analysis, literary theory, a question-answering guide, Macbeth revision, predictions, exam strategy, flashcards, a quiz, and a progress tracker.

Live: https://willemjvr.github.io/english-fal-p2-revision/
